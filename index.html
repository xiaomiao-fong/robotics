SAFMC 2026 — High-Speed Drone Flock

Project Lead · 2026

Simulation demo — full flock mission in Gazebo (video coming soon)

A system designed to race a flock of 2–3 drones through a gated indoor course autonomously, coordinated by a finite-state machine and a “Mediator” that schedules each drone and keeps the flock collision-free. As project lead, I directed full-system evaluation and design — avionics, software stack, and race strategy. The full autonomy stack is validated in Gazebo simulation, and we built and flight-tested the two-drone platform.
